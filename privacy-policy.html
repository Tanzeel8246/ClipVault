Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026. Your privacy and data security are our top priorities.

1. Introduction

Welcome to ClipVault, developed by MindCrafts_AI. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles clipboard data, inputs, voice typing, and backup operations. ClipVault is designed as a local-first productivity utility, meaning we prioritize keeping your private data strictly on your physical device.

2. Clipboard Monitoring & Keystroke Input

Because modern versions of Android (Android 10 and above) restrict background applications from accessing clipboard data, ClipVault provides a custom Input Method / Keyboard service (VaultBoard).

  • Clipboard Auto-Save: When you enable and use the VaultBoard keyboard, any text copy actions you perform are detected and stored automatically inside the application's local SQLite database.
  • Keystroke Tracking: VaultBoard processes your keystrokes purely on-device to input text into target text fields. We do not record, log, trace, or transmit your individual keystrokes or typing history to any remote server or third party.
  • Sensitive Data Masking: The app automatically detects sensitive details like One-Time Passwords (OTPs), passwords, and credit card numbers. By default, these clips are visually masked in the application and settings can be set to auto-delete them after a short period.
Important Note on Privacy: All clipboard capture and processing occur 100% locally. We do not maintain any cloud databases that store your copied clips.

3. Microphone & Voice Typing

ClipVault offers a voice-to-text (voice typing) function via the microphone button on the custom keyboard.

  • On-Device Speech Processing: When you use voice typing, your voice input is processed locally or using the native Android SpeechRecognizer APIs.
  • No Audio Storage: Audio recordings are processed in real-time to generate text and are never stored or uploaded to MindCrafts_AI servers. Voice data remains completely volatile and is discarded instantly after transcription.

4. Google Drive Cloud Backup

ClipVault offers an optional cloud backup service using your Google Drive account.

  • Isolated App Folder: If you explicitly log in via Google Sign-In and enable backup, your clipboard database is backed up to a dedicated folder in your Google Drive storage (specifically the drive.appdata scope).
  • Hidden from View: This directory is completely hidden from standard Google Drive browser views and can only be accessed by ClipVault.
  • Zero Developer Access: MindCrafts_AI has no server-side access to your Google credentials, auth tokens, or backups. The connection is direct between your mobile device and Google's servers.

5. Security, Passwords & Biometrics

To secure your saved clipboard items, you can enable the application lock using your device's biometric security (fingerprint/face recognition) or system security PIN/password.

  • System-Managed Security: ClipVault uses Android's native Keyguard and BiometricPrompt APIs. The app never collects, accesses, or stores your biometric prints or system passwords. All validation is handled securely by the Android OS.

6. Data Deletion and Control

You have full, absolute control over your data:

  • Local Wipe: You can clear individual clips, delete categories, or execute a "Panic Clean" (to instantly delete all flagged sensitive clips) in Settings.
  • Google Drive Removal: You can disconnect your Google account from the app Settings at any time, which halts auto-backups. You can also purge the backups stored in Google Drive.

7. Browser Extension (Urdu Customizer & Sync)

ClipVault offers a companion desktop browser extension. Its core functionalities operate fully locally, prioritizing your privacy:

  • Font Customization: The extension modifies website fonts to display Nastaleeq or other Urdu fonts locally on your computer. It does not send website content or browsing history to any remote server.
  • Clipboard Capture: Clips copied on your computer are saved strictly to the extension's local storage in your browser.
  • Cloud Syncing: If you explicitly sign in via Google, the extension syncs your local clipboard with your mobile app via an isolated Google Drive AppData folder. This is a direct device-to-Google connection with zero developer access.
  • Screen Recording: Screen capture or video recording functionalities operate entirely locally within your browser tab. Recordings are saved directly to your local downloads folder and are never uploaded to any cloud server.
